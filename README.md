## 🧠 What I Believe

```
Action > Anxiety
Awareness > Self-deception
Real experiences > Career planning
Product value > Tech showoff
Systems thinking > Inspiration-driven
```

- **Tool combos > Swiss army knife** — You don't cook every dish with one knife. You need a cleaver, a wok, and a steamer, each doing its job.
- **Being cost-conscious isn't cheap** — ¥55/month AI tool stack covers 90% of my needs. The money saved buys a year of coffee-shop coding sessions.
- **Don't let AI FOMO kidnap you** — What matters is what you *build* with AI, not how many tools you subscribe to.
- **The future belongs to one-person companies** — What you offer the world is a production system with your own style.
